### ◇ Nexus

**Your passwords. Your device. Your control.**

Nexus is a **local-first password manager** built around a simple principle — your passwords belong to you.

Your passwords are stored **locally on your device**. They are never uploaded to or stored on Zunix servers.

No password cloud. No remote password database. Your vault stays with you.
